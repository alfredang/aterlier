01 Philosophy

We design quietly, so the space can speak.

Atelier is a twelve-person practice working at the intersection of interior architecture and material culture. We believe a room should feel inevitable — not styled. Every commission begins with a conversation, a slow look at how you live, and the patience to wait until the right answer arrives.

— M. Devereux Founder & Principal
