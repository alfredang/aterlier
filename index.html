一 Philosophy

We design with the space between things.

SUI·AN — 翠庵, the emerald hermitage — is a twelve-person practice at the intersection of interior architecture and Eastern material culture. We believe a room should feel placed rather than built. Every commission begins with a long look at how you move through a day, and the patience to wait until the right answer arrives — quietly, in its own time.

— K. Mori Founder & Atelier-Master

二 Practice

Four disciplines, one stillness.

邸宅

Residential Interiors

Houses, sukiya-inspired retreats, and city apartments composed for the rhythm of daily ritual — from joinery to bedding linen, drawn by one hand.

Explore the discipline →

持成し

Hospitality & Retail

Tea rooms, boutique hotels, and brand spaces tuned to light, threshold, and the choreography of welcome — omotenashi made spatial.

間取り

Spatial Composition

Architectural editing — flow, proportion, daylight, the negative space of ma. We begin with the plan because everything else inherits from it.

修繕

Restoration & Renewal
